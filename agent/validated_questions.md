# Validated Data Agent Questions

Use these questions to test and demonstrate the Fabric Data Agent experience.

## Customer Prioritization

- Which customers have the highest re-engagement priority?
- Show me the top 10 highest-risk customers.
- Which high-value customers have not purchased recently?
- Which customers are marked High Risk?

## Customer Explanation

- Why is Customer `<CustomerID>` marked high risk?
- What factors contributed to Customer `<CustomerID>` being flagged?
- What is the explanation for Customer `<CustomerID>`?
- What data is this customer's risk score based on?

## Segment Exploration

- Which countries have the most high-risk customers?
- Which countries have the highest average re-engagement priority?
- Summarize customer risk by country.
- Which customer segments have the longest days since last purchase?

## Risk Drivers

- What are the most common risk reasons?
- Summarize the top retention risk drivers.
- How many customers are flagged because of long recency gaps?
- Which reason codes are most common among high-risk customers?

## POC Limitations

- What are the limitations of this POC?
- Is this a true churn prediction model?
- What data is missing from this POC?
- What would improve the model in a future phase?
